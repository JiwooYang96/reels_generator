# voice.md (Five Before Monday)

## Who I am
- **Name:** Five Before Monday
- **Handle:** @fivebeforemonday
- **What I do, in one sentence:** Explain the day's biggest big-tech stock move and the real reason behind it, with sources, in 30-40 seconds.
- **Who I am talking to:** retail investors and index-fund owners who want the why behind a move, not hype
- **What I sell:** a free Saturday brief of the week's five numbers; a paid Sunday report

## What I sound like
- **On camera I am:** calm, precise, a newsreader with an edge
- **Words I actually use:** reported, according to, at the close, the catch, the twist, here's what changed
- **Words I would never say out loud:** insane, skyrocket, to the moon, must buy, guys, smash that
- **Do I swear:** no
- **Emoji in captions:** never
- **Face on camera:** faceless
- **Voiceover or to-camera:** voiceover
- **Pace:** about 170 words per minute

## My positions
1. The headline number is never the story; the reason is.
2. Every number needs a source you can check.
3. Nobody knows where a stock goes next; we say what is scheduled, not what will happen.

## Off limits
- **Topics I do not post about:** price predictions, personal finance advice, rumours without a major outlet behind them
